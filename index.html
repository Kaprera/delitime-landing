What it does

WhatsApp order intake

Send the client a form link over WhatsApp. What they fill in arrives as a complete order, already in the queue.

Driver assignment

Operators pick the driver, with zones, availability, and distance in view. Reassign whenever the day changes.

Delivery status

New, assigned, in progress, complete — alongside cancels, refunds, and no-response passes, on one timeline.

Commissions and subscriptions

5% to the account, 1.5% to the platform, frozen against the price the order was priced at. Monthly subscription status per account.

Dashboards and PDF reports

Volume, revenue, and driver performance, filtered the way you work — plus a PDF report for every single order.

Roles and account isolation

Sysadmin, superadmin, admin, operator, driver. Enforced in PostgreSQL with row level security, not just in the UI.
